# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Aspiring home mixologists, complete beginners, working bartenders, and hospitality professionals looking to sharpen their craft. They visit to understand the academy's point of view, explore practical courses, meet the instructors, and choose a clear next step.

## Product Purpose

Skill & Mix turns serious cocktail education into a vivid, approachable cultural experience. The website should build desire, prove the academy's credibility, showcase its people and curriculum, and convert visitors into students or private-service clients.

## Brand Personality

Playful, experimental, and tactile. The brand should carry the raw digital-collage energy, oversized typography, energetic color, and surprising image compositions of des-todes.su while remaining unmistakably about drinks, hospitality, and hands-on learning.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate training sites, polished luxury-hotel styling, dark speakeasy clichés, gold-on-black “premium” cocktail branding, and conventional hospitality-school templates. Avoid anonymous card grids and stock imagery that feels staged or sterile.

## Design Principles

1. Make learning feel physical: show ingredients, glassware, hands, motion, and process.
2. Teach with personality: expert information should feel direct, generous, and alive.
3. Let every page earn its own rhythm while preserving a recognizable visual system.
4. Keep conversion paths obvious even when the art direction is playful.
5. Design for both first-timers and working professionals without talking down to either.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility target was requested. Preserve semantic structure, keyboard access, readable contrast, useful alt text, and sensible focus states as baseline product quality.
